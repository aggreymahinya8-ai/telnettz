About TEL.NET

Professional telecommunications and ICT solutions in Tanzania.

Our Company

TEL.NET SOLUTIONS COMPANY LIMITED is a telecommunications and ICT solutions company based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Established in 2026, the company is committed to delivering reliable, innovative and high-quality technology solutions. Our expertise includes fiber optic deployment, structured cabling, network infrastructure, CCTV surveillance, access control and ongoing technical maintenance.

Our Approach

Backed by skilled and energetic engineers, we focus on professionalism, quality workmanship, timely project execution and customer satisfaction. We work with telecom operators, banks, businesses, government institutions, offices and residential clients to deliver practical and scalable technology solutions.

Our Vision

To become Tanzania's leading and most trusted telecommunications and ICT infrastructure company by delivering innovative, reliable and high-quality connectivity, networking and security solutions.

Our Mission

To provide professional and reliable fiber optic, networking and security system solutions through quality workmanship, skilled engineering, timely project execution and exceptional customer service.
